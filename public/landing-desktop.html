Everything you need

Built for how squads actually travel.

Six tools, one trip. Each one is the simple version you wished existed when you were sharing a cabin with six people and someone owed someone for the third Uber.

01 · Money

Split expenses, settle before you land.

Log a cost, pick who's in, done. SquadStay does the maths and tells everyone exactly who pays whom — with the fewest possible transfers.

Cab from airportJM paid · split 5−£24
Groceries day 1SK paid · split 4−£18

Dinner AlfamaMN paid · split 5+£12
02 · Air

Flights, all in one place.

Drop your booking ref or forward the confirmation. Everyone's outbound and return flights, gate-tracked.

03 · Stay

Stay details that don't get lost.

Address, check-in window, Wi-Fi password, gate code — once. Maps link is one tap when you land.

04 · Discover

Places to go, built by you.

Save bars, beaches, viewpoints, that bakery your friend insists on. Everyone in the squad can drop pins. The map fills up as you all explore.

05 · Plan

An itinerary that survives reality.

Drag a stop, swap a day, mark something tentative. Re-orders survive a missed train.

06 · Squad

Invite the crew, set who edits.

Email invites, magic links, role per person. Your aunt can view; your co-planner can edit.

Settling up · the boring math, gone

Three transfers. Squad-wide. Done.

When you settle, SquadStay doesn't have everyone paying everyone. It calculates the minimum number of transfers and tells each person one thing to do. Five people, three taps, even.
